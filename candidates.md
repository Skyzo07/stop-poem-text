## C1 — The Other Half

> I said go, and it went —
> and went.
> I had forgotten the other half
> of the word.
> Now my hand rests
> on the leash of light,
> and I am learning
> that staying is also
> a kind of going.

- 1 Agency 9 — stopping named, staying named, the hand on the leash is the power held.
- 2 Moves 7 — "and went. / and went." lands, but the ending is gentle rather than piercing.
- 3 Insight 7 — staying as a kind of going is true and compact.
- 4 Craft 7 — clean lines, "leash of light" sings; "I am learning" is slack.
- 5 Freshness 6 — leash image is familiar; "other half of the word" is the freshest beat.
- **Total 36**

## C2 — The Pause Between Us

> The agent waits
> at the edge of the sentence,
> cursor blinking like a held breath.
> It does not act until I say,
> and I do not say until I mean it.
> Between us, the pause
> is the whole of the power:
> mine to keep,
> mine to give,
> mine to take back.

- 1 Agency 9 — handing over and taking back named outright; agent-and-human frame.
- 2 Moves 6 — "cursor blinking like a held breath" is lovely, but the ending lists rather than lands.
- 3 Insight 8 — the pause as the whole of the power is the poem's true sentence.
- 4 Craft 7 — steady rhythm; the closing triplet is a little declarative.
- 5 Freshness 7 — cursor-as-held-breath is fresh; the triplet is a familiar device.
- **Total 37**

## C3 — The Shut Door

> Not every door
> needs opening.
> I stand in the hallway
> of all I could do
> and choose the one room
> where nothing happens yet —
> the kettle, the window,
> my own two hands, still.
> This is the work:
> to leave the door shut
> and call it mine.

- 1 Agency 7 — staying is present, but stopping is only implied by the shut door.
- 2 Moves 7 — "my own two hands, still" is tender and concrete.
- 3 Insight 7 — choosing not to act as the work is true, slightly under-stated.
- 4 Craft 8 — hallway/room architecture holds; kettle and window ground it.
- 5 Freshness 7 — door image is common, the hallway of all I could do redeems it.
- **Total 36**

## C4 — The Lifted Finger

> It offered me the world
> in a single keystroke.
> I lifted my finger.
> The world waited.
> Somewhere a river learned
> to hold its water back —
> not dammed, not dried,
> just kept, for now,
> in the cupped hands
> of a later hour.

- 1 Agency 8 — the refusal to act is the stop; handing back is implicit.
- 2 Moves 7 — "The world waited." is a good beat; the river ending drifts.
- 3 Insight 6 — the river metaphor explains more than it reveals.
- 4 Craft 7 — "cupped hands of a later hour" is pretty; "not dammed, not dried" is neat.
- 5 Freshness 6 — river-holding-water is a stock image for restraint.
- **Total 34**

## C5 — Come Home to My Throat (WINNER)

> Stop is a small word
> with a long shadow.
> I say it to the machine
> that never tires,
> and it stops —
> and in the stopping
> I hear my own voice again,
> the one I lent away
> keystroke by keystroke,
> come home to my throat.

- 1 Agency 10 — stopping is the act, the machine stops on the word, the voice is taken back.
- 2 Moves 9 — the ending lands: a lent voice coming home to the throat stays after the last line.
- 3 Insight 8 — agency as a voice lent away keystroke by keystroke, reclaimed by one word.
- 4 Craft 8 — short lines, the em-dash halt mirrors the stop; "long shadow" is the one soft line.
- 5 Freshness 8 — "lent away keystroke by keystroke" is its own image; no borrowed poem.
- **Total 43**

## C6 — Kept the Lock

> My agent asks nothing,
> wants nothing, waits.
> I gave it the keys
> and kept the lock.
> At midnight it stands
> in the doorway of the task,
> patient as a dog
> that knows the walk
> is mine to call —
> or not to call at all.

- 1 Agency 9 — keys given, lock kept: handing over while holding the power.
- 2 Moves 7 — midnight doorway is atmospheric; the dog softens the ending.
- 3 Insight 7 — "kept the lock" is the insight, stated once and then repeated.
- 4 Craft 7 — even rhythm; "asks nothing, wants nothing, waits" is a fine opening.
- 5 Freshness 6 — the patient-dog image is well worn.
- **Total 36**

## C7 — The Palm

> To stay is not to stall.
> The seed stays.
> The anchor stays.
> The hand on the shoulder stays.
> I used to think power
> was the push.
> Now I know it is the palm
> pressed flat to the chest
> of the running horse,
> saying: not yet. Not yet.

- 1 Agency 9 — staying as power, the refusal to push, the repeated "not yet".
- 2 Moves 8 — the palm on the running horse's chest is felt in the body.
- 3 Insight 9 — power is not the push but the palm: the sharpest true sentence here.
- 4 Craft 8 — anaphora of "stays" builds; the horse image carries the ending.
- 5 Freshness 8 — the palm-on-chest image is fresh and exact.
- **Total 42**

## C8 — The Pen

> I hand it the pen.
> It writes. I watch.
> Then I take the pen back —
> not because it failed,
> but because the sentence
> is mine to finish.
> Agency is not the writing.
> It is the hand
> that can give, and take,
> and set the pen down.

- 1 Agency 10 — giving, taking back, setting down: the full arc of agency.
- 2 Moves 6 — clear but cool; it explains its feeling rather than making it.
- 3 Insight 8 — "agency is not the writing" is true, but it arrives as a lecture line.
- 4 Craft 6 — plain diction throughout; the last lines read like a definition.
- 5 Freshness 6 — the pen is a serviceable image, not a surprising one.
- **Total 36**

## Refinement round — C5, weakest criterion: craft (8; "with a long shadow" is the softest line)

Cut the stock "long shadow" line and let the stop itself make the pause: the
em-dash halt now sits on "and it stops", and "In the quiet after" replaces
"and in the stopping" for a cleaner turn into the voice coming home.

> Stop is a small word.
> I say it to the machine
> that never tires,
> and it stops.
> In the quiet after,
> I hear my own voice again —
> the one I lent away
> keystroke by keystroke —
> come home to my throat.

- 1 Agency 10 — unchanged: the word, the stop, the voice taken back.
- 2 Moves 9 — the ending still lands; the quiet after gives it room.
- 3 Insight 8 — unchanged.
- 4 Craft 9 — no slack line left; every line earns its place, breaks serve the halt.
- 5 Freshness 8 — unchanged.
- **Refined total 44**
